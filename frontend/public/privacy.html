SatoshiCore Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Publication item: Add SatoshiCore's public privacy contact email and legal operator details before submitting the app for Google verification.

This policy explains how SatoshiCore Messenger handles account information, messages, calls, and information accessed when you choose to sync Google Contacts. Contact syncing is optional and is not required to sign in or use messaging.

Information used to provide the service

SatoshiCore processes the account details you provide, profile information, messages and media you send, contact relationships, call signaling and call history, and technical information needed to operate and secure the service. The service uses this information to authenticate accounts, deliver requested features, prevent abuse, and maintain service reliability.

Google sign-in

When you choose Continue with Google, Google Identity Services returns an identity credential to your browser. SatoshiCore sends that credential to its server for verification and uses the verified Google account subject to sign you in. Google sign-in does not request access to your Google Contacts. SatoshiCore does not use email-address equality alone to merge an existing account.

Optional Google Contacts sync

Contacts sync is a separate action. When you select Sync Google Contacts, SatoshiCore requests the read-only contacts.readonly permission. It does not request permission to edit or delete your Google Contacts. The People API request asks for contact resource metadata, phone numbers, and email addresses; it does not request contact names or photos.

  • The browser uses the Google access token to retrieve contact pages from the Google People API. The access token is kept in browser memory and is not sent to SatoshiCore's application server.
  • For matching, the browser sends SatoshiCore only Google contact resource references, eligible E.164 phone numbers, email addresses, and deletion indicators over HTTPS.
  • SatoshiCore compares those values against active, verified members who have allowed contact discovery. Unmatched phone numbers and email addresses are processed for the request and are not saved as contact details.
  • SatoshiCore stores matched member IDs and Google resource references so that matches can be displayed and updated. It stores the People API sync cursor encrypted at rest.
  • Contact information is not sent to Gemini or another AI provider for this matching feature.

You can disconnect Google Contacts in the Contacts settings. Disconnecting removes the saved match results and sync cursor from SatoshiCore and requests Google to revoke the browser access token when one is available. You can also revoke SatoshiCore access from your Google Account permissions page.

Sharing and service providers

SatoshiCore shares information with Google only as needed for the Google sign-in or People API actions you initiate, and with infrastructure providers that operate the service under their own terms and security controls. SatoshiCore does not sell personal information. The app does not send contact lists to its AI features.

Retention and security

Message and account data are retained to provide the service and are subject to the service's account and message controls. Google Contacts match records and sync cursors remain while Contacts sync is connected and are removed when you disconnect. SatoshiCore uses access controls, encrypted transport, and encryption for stored sync cursors; no Internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may decline Google Contacts permission and still add people using their public SatoshiCore ID or QR code. You may disconnect Contacts sync at any time. You may also use your Google Account security settings to review or revoke the access granted to SatoshiCore.

Changes and contact

SatoshiCore may update this policy when its services or legal requirements change. The updated date above identifies the current version. Before public release, SatoshiCore will publish a monitored privacy contact address here for privacy requests and questions.
